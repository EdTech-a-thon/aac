# AAC

Augmentative and alternative communication product where people manage vocabularies and use them to communicate. The same person may do both.

## Language

**User**:
A person with an Auth account (email/password). Not split into separate account types.
_Avoid_: Manager account, AAC User account, role-as-account-type

**Vocabulary**:
A curated collection of Boards and Snippets that Users can manage and/or use, via separate relationships. It appears in the manager app for its Managers, in the AAC app for its Communicators and its Managers, to anyone holding a Share Link for it, and on the Gallery while it has a published Publication. Has a name, which may be blank (shown as an Untitled placeholder in the UI), and an optional description, which may be blank whether or not it is published. Publishing requires both to be non-blank and at least one Board. In the AAC app, Vocabularies a Communicator can use are listed alphabetically by that display name. Has exactly one Palette. Created with an Initial Snapshot of its Boards, Snippets, Buttons, Snippet Inclusions, and Palette. A duplicated Vocabulary is independent: its Initial Snapshot is the source Vocabulary's visible state at duplication, including staged edits when duplication begins in its editor, while those source edits remain unapplied; its Change Set history starts empty, and the duplicating User is its sole Manager and therefore its only Communicator. A Vocabulary a Visitor saves from a Share Link is a duplicate in exactly this sense; one copied from a Publication is too, except that it durably records the Publication Version it came from. That origin is a dead reference shown only to its own Managers: it never updates the copy, and it simply stops resolving if that Publication is withdrawn or its source Vocabulary deleted. Board order is unspecified except for the Home Board. Deleting a Vocabulary removes its Boards, Snippets, Buttons, Snippet Inclusions, Palette, Initial Snapshot, Change Sets, Management/Usage relationships with it, and its Publication with every Publication Version, Endorsement, Copy, and Report belonging to it. Symbols are not part of a Vocabulary and are not removed.
_Avoid_: Page set, word list

**Initial Snapshot**:
The full Boards, Snippets, Buttons, Snippet Inclusions, and Palette state recorded when a Vocabulary is created. For a blank Vocabulary, that means no Boards/Snippets/Buttons/Snippet Inclusions and a Fitzgerald-default Palette. Later, creation from a template or by duplicating a Vocabulary will also produce an Initial Snapshot. Immutable after creation. The live Vocabulary's Boards, Snippets, Buttons, Snippet Inclusions, and Palette are always reconstructable from its Initial Snapshot plus its Applied Change Sets in order.
_Avoid_: Baseline, seed, genesis, starting state (as a separate noun), template (as this concept)

**Palette**:
The Vocabulary's ordered set of Palette Colors available for Buttons. Order is meaningful (picker and settings present Palette Colors in that order) and may be changed through Change Sets. May be empty. A blank Vocabulary's Initial Snapshot includes a Fitzgerald-default Palette whose Palette Colors use grammatical-category names, short “when to choose this” descriptions, and the corresponding hexes. Editing a Palette Color's hex changes the displayed color of every Button bound to that Palette Color. After creation, Palette changes are made through Change Sets (Applied or Suggested), not by editing the Initial Snapshot.
_Avoid_: Theme, color scheme, Fitzgerald key (as the name of this concept)

**Palette Color**:
An entry in a Palette: a CSS hex (`#RRGGBB`), an optional short name (e.g. noun, verb), and an optional longer description of when to use it. Distinct from a Button's custom hex, which is not a Palette Color. Within one Palette, hexes may repeat and names may repeat (including multiple blank names). Deleting a Palette Color that no Button is bound to simply removes it. Deleting one that Buttons are bound to requires a resolution in the same Change Set: reassign those Buttons to another background color (another Palette Color, a custom hex, or unset/None), or convert each to a custom hex equal to the deleted Palette Color's last hex (freeze appearance). If a Suggested Change Set deletes a Palette Color that was unused when suggested, and Buttons are bound to it by the time the Change Set is Applied, those Buttons convert to that custom-hex freeze (no further prompt).
_Avoid_: Swatch, color token, named color (as a separate noun from Palette Color)

**Board**:
A grid that belongs to exactly one Vocabulary, with a width and height (columns and rows) describing its visible viewport. Width and height are integers ≥ 1. Has a name, which may be blank (shown as an Untitled placeholder in the UI). Boards are not shared across Vocabularies; reuse is by copy. Ordering among Boards in a Vocabulary is unspecified for now. Viewport coordinates are 0-based: in-viewport columns are `0 .. width-1`, rows `0 .. height-1`. A Board may hold Snippet Inclusions. A Board may have a Share Link. A Board cannot outlive its Vocabulary; deleting a Board removes its Buttons, its Snippet Inclusions, and its Share Link — the included Snippets themselves remain.
_Avoid_: Page, screen, grid (as the name of this concept)

**Board Copy**:
A new independent Board, created immediately through one Applied Change Set, containing all of a source Board's visible-state Buttons and Snippet Inclusions without applying pending source edits; content outside its viewport and relative overlap order are preserved. Within one Vocabulary it includes the existing live Snippets and retains Palette Color bindings and Open Board Actions; across Vocabularies it copies the included Snippets and their dependency closure, freezes Palette Color bindings as custom hexes, remaps self-opening Actions to the new Board, and clears Actions targeting other Boards.
_Avoid_: Board clone, shared Board, duplicate Board (as the name of this concept)

**Unresolved Copy Action**:
A Manager-only warning attached to a copied Button whose Open Board Action was cleared because its target was not copied into the destination Vocabulary. It remains until a Manager assigns a valid Action or deletes the Button; it cannot be dismissed while the Button remains unresolved, is not an Action, and is not visible in the AAC app.
_Avoid_: Broken link, invalid Action, dangling Action

**Home Board**:
The Board a Communicator enters when opening a Vocabulary that has at least one Board. It is the earliest-created Board (oldest creation time; if tied, the one with the lower identifier). A Vocabulary with no Boards has no Home Board. Snippets are not Boards and cannot be a Home Board. Manager-chosen Home Board is unspecified for now. The AAC app’s **Home** control opens the Home Board (no-op if already there); it is not a Button Action and does not use a Board history stack.
_Avoid_: Start board, main board, default board, first board (as the name of this concept)

**Snippet**:
A grid that belongs to exactly one Vocabulary, with a width and height (columns and rows) describing its visible viewport, the same way a Board does. Width and height are integers ≥ 1. Has a name, which may be blank (shown as an Untitled placeholder in the UI). A Snippet is not a Board: it is never a Home Board and never an Open Board target. Snippets are not shared across Vocabularies; reuse is by copy. Viewport coordinates are 0-based: in-viewport columns are `0 .. width-1`, rows `0 .. height-1`. A Snippet may hold Snippet Inclusions of other Snippets, but must not include itself, directly or through a chain of inclusions. A Snippet cannot outlive its Vocabulary; deleting a Snippet removes its Buttons, the Snippet Inclusions it holds, and every Snippet Inclusion of it. Converting a Board into a Snippet or a Snippet into a Board is unspecified for now.
_Avoid_: Template, live template, overlay, popup, word list, widget, inset, shared board

**Snippet Inclusion**:
A placement of a Snippet on a Board or on another Snippet, at an origin (0-based row and column). It occupies a rectangle of the included Snippet's width × height from that origin. The rectangle may sit partly or wholly outside the host's viewport; only the intersection is shown and hittable, and the inclusion remains part of the host. Every inclusion of the same Snippet is that same live Snippet. The same Snippet may be included more than once on the same host. Deleting a Snippet Inclusion removes that placement only; the Snippet remains. Contents of a Snippet are edited only on that Snippet's own canvas. A Manager creates a Snippet Inclusion by choosing a Snippet and an origin cell that has no host Button and is not inside another inclusion's rectangle. Left-click on such an empty cell still creates a Button. An inclusion flattens to the included Snippet's own Buttons, then that Snippet's inclusions by the same rule (recursive). Empty cells in an inclusion are transparent: they are not a blank tile and older inclusions may show through. When several inclusions on the same host map a Button to the same coordinates, the most recently created inclusion wins (newest creation time; if tied, the one with the higher identifier). Host Buttons still beat all inclusion content, regardless of creation time. In the AAC app, draw and hit use that flattened result. In the manager app, each inclusion is shown as a distinct layer over its rectangle with inner Buttons de-emphasized; clicking selects the newest inclusion whose rectangle contains the point, even if flattened content at that point comes from an older inclusion or is empty. A Manager opens a Snippet's canvas from the Snippets list, by double-clicking an inclusion of it, or from a control while that inclusion is selected.
_Avoid_: Embedding, instance, stamp, widget, overlay, nested board

**Button**:
An occupant on a Board or a Snippet, identified by 0-based row and column indices, with a label that may be blank and a background color that is unset, a binding to one Palette Color in its Vocabulary's Palette, or a custom CSS hex (`#RRGGBB`) not stored as a Palette Color. Unset is the default for new Buttons and can be chosen again later; when unset, the Button is rendered as white. In Manager UI, the unset option is labeled **None**. A custom hex is never auto-bound to a Palette Color just because the paint matches; binding only happens when a Manager chooses that Palette Color. Empty cells in that Board's or Snippet's viewport are the absence of a Button belonging to that Board or Snippet in that cell, not a blank Button. A host cell with no Button of its own may still show a Button from a Snippet Inclusion. Multiple Buttons on the same Board or on the same Snippet may share the same coordinates; Buttons may sit outside their Board's or Snippet's width/height viewport and remain part of it. When Buttons on the same Board or the same Snippet overlap, draw and hit order are the same: the most recently created Button wins (newest creation time; if tied, the one with the higher identifier). When a Button belonging to the host and content from a Snippet Inclusion map to the same coordinates, the host Button is drawn and hit in the AAC app, and is selected in preference to the inclusion in the manager app. A Button may have zero or one Action, and zero or one Symbol; label and Symbol are independent and either may be absent.
_Avoid_: Cell, tile, key

**Symbol**:
The image shown on a Button, identified by an immutable digest of its bytes. Not Vocabulary data: the same Symbol is shared by every Button that names it, in any Vocabulary, and it outlives the Buttons, Boards, Snippets, and Vocabularies that reference it. Uploaded outside Change Sets — a Change Set only sets a Button's reference to one. Drawn below the Button's label, sized to fit entirely within the Button without cropping, with the Button's background color showing behind it, so a Symbol with transparency takes the color of its Button. The label's space is reserved even when the label is blank, so Symbols stay aligned across a grid.
_Avoid_: Icon, Pictogram, Picture, Image, Image library, Button Image

**Message Bar**:
The composition strip in the AAC app where the user builds a message while using a Vocabulary. It is an ordered list of phrases appended by Insert Phrase Actions, shown and spoken as those phrases joined by a single space. Clear Message Bar empties the list; Backspace removes the last phrase. It survives Open Board within a Vocabulary session and is discarded when leaving that Vocabulary, signing out, or the AAC app process ending — not persisted, not part of durable Vocabulary/Board/Button data, and not present in the manager app.
_Avoid_: Sentence bar, speech bar, utterance bar, compose bar

**Speak**:
The AAC app control that speaks the current Message Bar (phrases joined by a single space). Empty Message Bar: no-op. A new Speak or Speak Immediately stops current speech and starts the new utterance. Not a Button Action and not part of Vocabulary data. Distinct from Speak Immediately, which is an Action that speaks a configured phrase without using the Message Bar.
_Avoid_: Speak Message Bar (as an Action kind), Play, TTS, Speak button (as a Board Button)

**Action**:
What happens when a Button is pressed in the AAC app. Configuring an Action (in the manager app) is separate from performing it. A Button may have no Action; when present, it is a single value discriminated by kind. Kinds: **Insert Phrase** (adds a configured non-empty phrase to the Message Bar; phrase is separate from the Button's label), **Speak Immediately** (speaks a configured non-empty phrase without changing the Message Bar; phrase is separate from the Button's label), **Open Board** (jumps to a specified Board in the same Vocabulary, replacing the current Board with no Board history stack; if that Board is deleted, the Button's Action is cleared), **Play YouTube Clip** (plays a YouTube video segment identified by video id plus start and end times in seconds, which may be fractional, with start before end), **Clear Message Bar** (empties the Message Bar), and **Backspace** (removes the last phrase from the Message Bar). Incomplete Actions are not persisted — required payload fields must be present and valid, or the Button has no Action.
_Avoid_: Click action, press behavior, on-click, button behavior, Speak Phrase, Navigate to Board, Go to Board, Play video, Clear, Delete

**Management relationship**:
A many-to-many link between a User and a Vocabulary granting permission to configure that vocabulary (what the manager app shows). Creating a Vocabulary creates a Management relationship for the creator. Additional Managers can be added by an existing Manager supplying another User's email; if no User has that email, the add fails. Managers can remove Management relationships, but a Vocabulary must always retain at least one Manager.
_Avoid_: Ownership (for this relationship), editor, vocabulary editor, pending invite (for now)

**Usage relationship**:
A many-to-many link between a User and a Vocabulary granting permission to communicate with that vocabulary (what the app shows): read the live Vocabulary (Boards, Snippets, Buttons, Snippet Inclusions, Palette as reconstructed from Initial Snapshot plus Applied Change Sets), perform Actions, and use the Message Bar. Not Suggested Change Sets, Applied history, or any durable writes. Management entails Usage: every Manager of a Vocabulary may communicate with it without holding a Usage relationship, so creating or duplicating a Vocabulary does not create one. A User may still hold an explicit Usage relationship as well as Management, but it grants nothing further. Communicators can be added by an existing Manager supplying another User's email; if no User has that email, the add fails. Communicators cannot add Usage relationships. Managers can remove Usage relationships, including the last one; Communicators cannot, including their own. Removing a Manager's explicit Usage relationship does not take away their access — that requires removing their Management relationship. A Vocabulary may have zero Usage relationships; its Managers can still communicate with it. Managers of a Vocabulary can see its Communicators. A Communicator can see their own Usage relationship, not other Communicators or the Managers. Removing a Usage relationship takes effect when the Communicator next lists or opens Vocabularies; an already-open Vocabulary session continues until they leave it.
_Avoid_: Assignment, subscription

**Share Link**:
A revocable capability granting anonymous read of one Vocabulary or one Board to whoever holds it. Any Manager of the Vocabulary may create or revoke one; there is at most one live Share Link per Vocabulary and per Board, and revoking it makes that link dead, so re-sharing produces a different one. It shows the live Vocabulary rather than a frozen copy, so Change Sets applied after sharing are visible through it. A Vocabulary Share Link covers all of that Vocabulary's Boards, Snippets, Buttons, Snippet Inclusions, and Palette. A Board Share Link covers that Board, the Snippets it needs to render including their own inclusions, and the Palette Colors its Buttons bind to; Open Board Actions targeting Boards outside the link do nothing and do not disclose their targets. Distinct from a Publication in every respect that matters: a Share Link is unguessable, live, and for distribution the Manager controls, where a Publication is discoverable, frozen, and public. A Vocabulary may have both at once, independently. Not Vocabulary data: made outside Change Sets, never carried by duplication or Board Copy, and destroyed with the Vocabulary or Board it names.
_Avoid_: Public Vocabulary, published Board, shared Board, invite link, guest access, permalink, Publication

**Manager** (role via relationship):
A User acting through a Management relationship to a Vocabulary — not a separate kind of account. Managing a Vocabulary means managing all of its Boards and Snippets, and also communicating with it.
_Avoid_: Caregiver, clinician, admin (as account types), editor, board editor, board manager, vocabulary editor

**Communicator** (role via relationship):
A User acting through a Usage relationship to a Vocabulary, or any Manager of it — not a separate kind of account. Communicating with a Vocabulary means using the live Vocabulary in the AAC app.
_Avoid_: AAC User (as a role name), client, speaker, end user

**Visitor** (role via Share Link):
A person following a Share Link or previewing a Publication on the Gallery, whether or not they hold a User account or any relationship to that Vocabulary. A Visitor sees what the link covers as a Manager would see it and may edit it; those edits are theirs alone, never become Change Sets on the source, and last only as long as their own browser holds them. A Visitor cannot submit Change Sets, add Symbols, or see Suggested Change Sets, Applied history, Unresolved Copy Actions, Managers, or Communicators. A Visitor may discard their edits in one confirmed step, restoring what they were shown — the current live state for a Share Link, which may have moved on since they began, and that Publication Version for a Publication. Signing in lets a Visitor keep what they see: a shared Vocabulary is saved as a new independent Vocabulary of their own, and a shared Board as a Board Copy into a new or existing Vocabulary of theirs. Keeping a Publication is recorded as a Copy; keeping from a Share Link is not recorded at all.
_Avoid_: Guest, anonymous user, public user, viewer, recipient, unauthenticated Communicator

**Change Set**:
A submitted collection of mutations to a Vocabulary's Boards, Snippets, Buttons, Snippet Inclusions, and/or Palette. Belongs to exactly one Vocabulary; it does not outlive that Vocabulary. Has an author: the User who submitted it. Only a Manager of that Vocabulary may submit a Change Set, as Applied or as Suggested — non-Managers cannot submit Change Sets. Does not include Vocabulary name changes, Management/Usage relationship changes, or changes to the Initial Snapshot — those stay outside Change Sets (the Initial Snapshot is fixed at creation). Each Change Set has a status: **Applied** (its mutations are part of the live Vocabulary) or **Suggested** (saved, not yet applied). Applying a Change Set is the only durable way Boards, Snippets, Buttons, Snippet Inclusions, or Palette Colors are created, updated, or deleted after creation. Symbols are the exception that proves it: they are not Vocabulary data, their bytes are uploaded directly, and a Change Set carries only a Button's reference to one. Submitting as Applied applies immediately and appends that Change Set to the Vocabulary's Applied history. Applying a Suggested Change Set flips that same Change Set to Applied and appends it as the new tip of the Applied history; any Manager of that Vocabulary may apply it. Any Manager may delete a Suggested Change Set without applying it. Applied Change Sets for a Vocabulary form an ordered, permanent history (for the life of the Vocabulary); the live Boards, Snippets, Buttons, Snippet Inclusions, and Palette are always reconstructable from the Initial Snapshot plus that sequence. Suggested Change Sets are not part of that permanent history — they may be updated or deleted when an Applied Change Set lands and they are no longer meaningful (including deletion when nothing in them would do anything anymore). The exact update rules for Suggested Change Sets are unspecified for now. Suggested Change Sets are not tied to a specific point in the Applied history. “Suggestion” means a Change Set with Suggested status — not a separate concept.
_Avoid_: Draft, draft vocabulary, batch, pending edits, working copy, autosave buffer, Suggestion (as a separate noun)

**Projected Vocabulary**:
The Boards, Snippets, Buttons, Snippet Inclusions, and Palette a Vocabulary would have if a sequence of not-yet-Applied mutations were Applied, in order, to the live Vocabulary. The live Vocabulary is unchanged; Vocabulary name, Management/Usage relationships, and the Initial Snapshot are not part of it.
_Avoid_: Replay, dry run, interpreter, preview state, working copy (as the name of this concept)

**Gallery**:
The public, sign-in-free surface where Publications are browsed, searched, previewed, and copied. Anonymous people may browse and preview; Endorsing and Copying require signing in. Served from the same public surface as Share Links, entered from a different route. Ordered by Endorsement count by default, with a newest ordering alongside it and substring search over Publication titles and descriptions. Records no views.
_Avoid_: Community platform, marketplace, store, library, directory, feed, hub

**Publication**:
A Vocabulary's durable presence on the Gallery, belonging to that Vocabulary rather than to the Manager who created it. Holds an ordered series of Publication Versions, its accumulated Endorsements, Copies, and Reports, and a stable human-readable slug derived from the Vocabulary's name at first publish and never changed afterwards, so links do not rot when the name does. Any Manager of the Vocabulary may publish or unpublish, without the other Managers' agreement. Unpublishing withdraws it: its Gallery entry and its slug go dead while every row survives, and publishing that Vocabulary again resumes the same Publication with its Endorsements and history intact. A Vocabulary may hold a Publication and a Share Link at the same time; they are independent. Destroyed only with its Vocabulary.
_Avoid_: Listing, post, submission, published vocabulary (as a separate noun), share link, public share

**Publication Version**:
An immutable snapshot of a Vocabulary published to the Gallery: its Boards, Snippets, Buttons, Snippet Inclusions, and Palette, together with the Vocabulary's name and description at that moment, its Attestation, an optional attribution string naming the publisher as they wish to be credited, and figures derived once at publish — board count, button count, and the range of Board grid sizes. Publishing again mints a new version; editing the Vocabulary, including its name or description, never changes a published one, so correcting a listing's prose means publishing again. The Gallery shows only its Publication's current version, and there is no rollback — a worse version is answered by publishing a better one. Earlier versions survive as the origin recorded on Copies made from them.
_Avoid_: Revision, release, edition, draft, snapshot (unqualified — see Initial Snapshot)

**Attestation**:
The record of what a Manager confirmed in order to publish: that they hold the right to share the content, that it will be free for anyone to copy and use, and that it contains no identifying content about a person who has not agreed to it. Stored per Publication Version, since consent to one version's content says nothing about another's, with each confirmation kept separately alongside the attesting User, the time, and the identifier of the exact consent wording shown — consent wordings being immutable records themselves. All three must be true to publish. The attesting User is retained even if that User is deleted or ceases to be a Manager.
_Avoid_: Consent, terms acceptance, licence, agreement, checkbox, disclaimer

**Endorsement**:
A User's public vote of confidence in a Publication, at most one per User per Publication and never on their own. It is a toggle whose history is kept rather than erased: un-endorsing records that it was withdrawn and when, and the count the Gallery shows is of Endorsements currently standing. Belongs to the Publication, not to a Publication Version. The count is public; who endorsed is not, to anyone, including the publisher. The Gallery's public ranking signal.
_Avoid_: Star, upvote, like, vote, favourite, rating, review

**Copy**:
The record of one Vocabulary having been created from a Publication, written when the copy completes rather than when it is requested, and naming the Publication, the Publication Version taken, the copying User, and the time. One row per resulting Vocabulary, so the same person copying repeatedly is counted each time. The count is shown to the Publication's Managers and to no one else; it is never public and is not a ranking signal. The resulting Vocabulary is a duplicate in the ordinary sense and is otherwise unaffected by anything that later happens to the Publication.
_Avoid_: Download, install, fork, clone, adoption, remix

**Report**:
What someone raises against a Publication from the Gallery, signed in or not, with a required short reason. Stored as a record naming the Publication, the reporter if known, the reason, the time, and whether it has been notified yet; the record is what matters and the email is only a nudge toward it, so a failed send never loses a Report. Notification is throttled rather than scheduled: a Report triggers an email only when none has been sent in the past hour, and that email covers every Report not yet notified rather than only the one that triggered it, so the throttle delays a Report without ever dropping one. A burst followed by silence accordingly leaves its tail unnotified until the next Report arrives. Reports do not withdraw a Publication; acting on one is a manual operator matter.
_Avoid_: Flag, takedown, abuse report, complaint, moderation queue
