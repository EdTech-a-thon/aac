# A Publication is a frozen series of versions, not a second Share Link

Submitting a Vocabulary to the Gallery mints a Publication Version: an immutable snapshot of its Boards, Snippets, Buttons, Snippet Inclusions, and Palette, together with the name, description, attribution, and Attestation as they stood at that moment, and figures derived once at publish. Editing the Vocabulary afterwards changes nothing on the Gallery until someone publishes again, which mints another version of the same Publication. This is the opposite of ADR 0010's Share Link, which is deliberately live, and the two are kept as separate concepts with separate routes rather than one mechanism with a flag: a Share Link is unguessable, live, and distributed by the Manager, while a Publication is guessable, frozen, and offered to strangers.

Freezing is what makes the rest of the feature coherent. The Attestation is a claim about specific content, and content that mutates underneath it makes the claim unfalsifiable. A Copy count against a moving target measures nothing. A Manager halfway through redesigning a vocabulary should not be exposing that state to a public gallery. And the snapshot is machinery that already exists — ADR 0003's Initial Snapshot and `create_vocabulary_from_snapshot` — so copying from the Gallery is the duplication path the codebase already has, not a new one.

A Publication belongs to the Vocabulary rather than to the Manager who created it, so it survives that Manager leaving, accumulates its Endorsements across every version, and resumes intact when a Vocabulary is unpublished and later published again. Unpublishing withdraws rather than deletes: the listing and slug go dead, every row survives. The slug is fixed at first publish and never tracks a later rename, so links that escaped keep working.

## Consequences

There is no rollback. The Gallery shows only the current version, and a version worse than its predecessor is answered by publishing a better one, not by reverting. Because name and description live on the Vocabulary and are captured at publish, fixing a typo in a listing's prose means publishing a fresh version whose content is identical to the last — accepted in exchange for having one source of truth and nothing to keep in sync. Managers must remember to re-publish after improving a Vocabulary, so the manager app has to tell them when a published Vocabulary has drifted from its current version.
