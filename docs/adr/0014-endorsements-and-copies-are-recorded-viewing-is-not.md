# Endorsements and Copies are recorded; Gallery viewing is not

ADR 0010 refused viewer analytics on Share Links, on the grounds that anonymous access which quietly logs who opened it is a surveillance surface the feature does not need. The Gallery keeps that refusal — no view counts, no visitor logs, nothing recorded for browsing or previewing a Publication — while deliberately recording two things that ADR 0010's reasoning does not cover. An Endorsement and a Copy are volitional acts by a signed-in User against an artifact its author chose to put on a public gallery, not passive observation of someone reading a private link. That is the whole of the distinction, and it is why this is a narrowing of ADR 0010 rather than a reversal of it.

The two are recorded differently because they answer different questions. An Endorsement is the Gallery's public ranking signal: one per User per Publication, never on one's own, a toggle whose withdrawal is recorded rather than erased so the history survives, with the standing count shown publicly and the endorsing identities shown to nobody — including the Publication's own Managers, for whom knowing which named clinicians endorsed their vocabulary has no upside. A Copy is the honest measure of use: one row per resulting Vocabulary, written on completion, naming the version taken and the copying User, counting repeats by the same person because a clinician making one copy per client is the signal rather than noise in it. Its aggregate is shown to that Publication's Managers as encouragement and to nobody else; the identities are operator-only.

## Consequences

The most trustworthy metric is deliberately unavailable for ranking, so the Gallery is ordered by Endorsements — a weaker signal, chosen because it is the one the author consented to being public. Refusing view counts also means Copies have no denominator: twelve copies cannot be told apart from twelve copies out of four thousand looks. Both are accepted. If reach is ever wanted, it should return as an explicit, aggregate-only, non-identifying counter and amend this decision rather than arrive quietly.
